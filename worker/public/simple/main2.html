Answers from IRS publications, with checked quotes

Ask a U.S. federal income tax question. Every answer quotes the publication it comes from, and code checks each quote word for word against the source before you see it. If the publications do not cover your question, it says so instead of guessing.

10 questions per visitor per day. Each live question costs about $0.0005 on Claude Haiku 5.5.
